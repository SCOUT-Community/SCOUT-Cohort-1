# SCOUT — Cohort 1

### Building Thinkers. Producing Evidence. Creating Opportunities.

Welcome to **SCOUT Cohort 1** — a practical Data Analysis and Research Training programme designed to help learners develop the skills to work with data, conduct meaningful research, communicate evidence, and solve real-world problems.

## About SCOUT

SCOUT is a learning community focused on **Data Analysis and Research**.

We believe that access to proper education, practical skills, mentorship, and opportunities can empower people to move from simply learning to actually creating evidence and impact.

## Cohort 1

Cohort 1 is the first SCOUT learning cohort, bringing together learners interested in developing practical skills in data analysis and research.

### Learning Tracks

#### 📊 Data Analysis
- Excel
- SQL
- Python
- Power BI

#### 🔬 Research
- Research Fundamentals
- Research Methodology
- Data Collection
- SPSS
- Research Writing
